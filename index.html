FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions patients ask most often.

How Can I Schedule an Appointment?−

Call during office hours or use our online booking system on the website.

What Should I Bring to My Appointment?+

Please bring your health card, photo identification, a list of your current medications, and any relevant medical records or referral documents.

How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?+

Contact the clinic as soon as possible during office hours. Our team will help you cancel or find another suitable appointment time.

What should I do in case of a medical emergency?+

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Do not use the online appointment form for urgent or life-threatening situations.

Is my medical information kept confidential?+

Yes. We protect your personal health information and only share it with your consent, or when disclosure is required by law.
